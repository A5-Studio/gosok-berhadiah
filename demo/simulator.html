Prize engine simulator

Runs the real pickPrize() port from mock-api.js (05_PrizePicker.gs) against a fresh copy of the CASH_PRIZES seed (5k×8666, 10k×2317, 20k×2000, 50k×500, 100k×150, 200k×50 = Rp 156,5 jt) with a seeded RNG. Each cash win applies the same inventory writes as redeemCardInner().

Drives tier and KPI views. The spend chart always shows both.

Cumulative cash spend per day: with vs without smoothing

Bars show scratches per day (right axis). Without per-day quotas the picker only gates on inventory, so a launch spike burns through the budget early. After that, every later scratch is forced into the draw pool (eligibleTiers.length === 0). With quotas, each day's tier cap is ceil(total_qty / days).
